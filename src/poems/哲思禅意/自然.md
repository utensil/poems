> 采撷意译创作自叶芝的诗：
>
> Then nowise worship dusty deeds,
>
> Nor seek, for this is also sooth,
>
> To hunger fiercely after truth.
>
> Lest all thy toiling only breeds
>
> New dreams, new dreams; there is no truth
>
> Saving in thine own heart.

何智逐尘功，
本真望莫渴。
求索生新梦，
实相在心豁。
